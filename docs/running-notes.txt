Goal: system-level PROACTIVE / PREVENTATIVE humanitarian intervention -- help people thrive on their land 

Guiding questions:
	1. Where are people struggling to survive in their current location / means
	2. Where are people at risk of forcible displacement 

Metrics to track:
For each, what are the drivers?
	What is the current level of risk from that driver?
	What is the rate of increase of risk from that driver?
	Where is risk projected to be in __ months?
	How many people are projected to be displaced?
	How many people are projected to face acute food insecurity / malnutr / starvation?
	How many people are projected to die?
	How does risk compare across the region?
	Where is aid needed the most/fastest?
	What kind of aid is needed?
	How much and what kind of aid is available?
	How much and what kind of aid is needed but unavailable?
	

Start with 2 - where are people at risk of forcible displacement?

	Driver_a = settler attacks 
		What is the current level of risk from that driver?
			- num/type/result of attacks in last 30 days (raw, per capita)
			- num/type/result attacks in last week
		What is the rate of increase of risk from that driver?
			- week over week rate of increase
			- month over month rate of increase
		Where is risk projected to be in __ months?
			- projected num of attacks next week (based on WOW trend)
			- projected number of attacks next month (based on MOM trend)
		How many people are projected to be displaced/ killed?

		How does risk compare across the region?
			- use a [combination of] metric(s) to create a quantifiable index / ranking 
		Where is aid needed the most/fastest?
			- rank by threat imminence

		What kind of aid is needed?
		How much and what kind of aid is available?
